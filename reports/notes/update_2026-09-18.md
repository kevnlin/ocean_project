## Slide 1 — The sanity check passes, so the problem is not the model

**Say:** the model can be driven below the target on data it is allowed to fit,
so capacity, tokenisation and the decoder all work. What it cannot do is
generalise to a float 150 km away — and neither can anything else.

Gulf Stream, 2 seeds (the gyre is in `overfit_sanity.md` and agrees):

| what was fitted | TEMP | SALT |
|---|---|---|
| one month, memorised | 0.017 z = **0.025 °C** | 0.014 z = **0.005 PSU** |
| the input profiles themselves (copy test) | 0.013 z = **0.022 °C** | 0.013 z = **0.004 PSU** |
| one month, with the audit fixes | 0.007 z = **0.012 °C** | 0.008 z = **0.003 PSU** |
| eight months | 0.106 z = **0.170 °C** | 0.100 z = **0.031 PSU** |

Show `fig_overfit.png`. One line: *the 0.1-0.2 °C target is reachable on fitted
data and not reachable at a held-out float, for reasons on slide 2.*

**This also settles the bigger-model question.** Capacity was never the binding
constraint, which is why widening the model degraded rather than helped. Keep
the 405 K configuration.

## Slide 2 — Why every number sits near 1

Show `fig_pair_correlation.png`.

* One z unit **is** the anomaly's own standard deviation: 1.6-2.1 °C in the Gulf
  Stream, ~1.0 °C in the gyre. RMSE ≈ 1 z means "predicts the training
  climatology".
* Two floats in the same month correlate ~0.75 when co-located and ~0 beyond
  150-200 km in the Gulf Stream. With the 128-profile cap the median held-out
  target sits **148 km** from its nearest input.
* The irreducible part (the nugget of the fitted covariance) is 0.36-0.45 of the
  variance, so **the best score any method can reach at a held-out float is
  J ≈ 0.5-0.65**, not J ≈ 0.1.

**Retire the 0.1-0.2 °C target** for real-float evaluation and replace it with a
skill score against that floor.

## Slide 3 — The baseline we were missing

**Say this first, before any table:** a kriging OI with zero trained parameters,
covariance fitted on the training years, scored on the identical held-out
floats:

| | TEMP J | SALT J |
|---|---:|---:|
| DFS (ours, 406 K, 12 k steps) — Gulf Stream | 0.961 | 0.991 |
| **kriging OI, same 128 profiles** | **0.956** | **0.976** |
| **kriging OI, every profile in the month** | **0.926** | **0.950** |
| DFS (ours) — N. Pacific gyre | 0.849 | 0.875 |
| **kriging OI, same 128 profiles** | **0.814** | **0.880** |
| **kriging OI, every profile** | **0.796** | **0.866** |

The `Causal OI` row in the current tables is not this: it is a kernel-weighted
mean with GODAS-era length scales and its background shrinkage switched off by
the noise values it is fed, which is why it scores *worse than climatology*.
Replace it.

## Slide 5 — Loss curves, and what the ablations say

Show `fig_loss_curves.png` and `fig_ablation.png`
(`reports/real_data/ablation_ladder.md` has the table; 17 arms x 2 seeds x 2
regions, 54 runs, no failures).

**What the curves say:** validation is **still falling at 12 000 steps** in every
arm — the runs in the tables are not converged, so part of the "insensitivity"
is a budget question. **What the ladder says:** the switches that make the model
*local* are worth −0.031 to −0.038 z; the fusion-rule switches (DFS / uniform /
count) sit inside the ±0.012 seed spread; and handing the baseline every profile
makes it **worse** (+0.010) while the same profiles are worth −0.038 to a
backbone that is local by construction.

Training curves come from `62_sanity_train.py`; every run logs to JSONL and to
W&B. The W&B runs are offline in `outputs/wandb/` — `wandb login` then
`wandb sync outputs/wandb/wandb/offline-run-*` puts them in the dashboard.

## Slide 6 — Where the headroom actually is

Profiles-only, the optimal linear estimate from the floats we hand the model is
J ≈ 0.93. The floor is J ≈ 0.6. **The difference is information the profiles do
not contain** — which is exactly what the satellite channels supply, and what
the main tables exclude by design.

Preliminary, secondary split (2016-2020 train / 2021 validate / 2022-23 test,
the years `data/real_obs_1deg.zarr` covers), Gulf Stream, 2 seeds:

| arm | test TEMP (z) | J |
|---|---:|---:|
| Perceiver-IO, profiles only | 1.0840 | 0.992 |
| SetConv-UNet, profiles only | 1.0662 | 0.975 |
| kriging OI, profiles only, every profile | 1.0015 | 0.916 |
| **SetConv-UNet + altimetry (SLA) only** | **0.9162** | **0.838** |
| **SetConv-UNet + satellite SST/SLA/SSS** | **0.8883** | **0.813** |

Same months, same floats, same 409 K size: the backbone swap alone changes
almost nothing, the extra modality moves J from 0.92 to 0.81 — the first
configuration in this project that clearly beats the interpolator. The gain is
**largest in the deepest band**, and the **altimetry-only** arm carries most of
it, which rules out the L4 SST/SSS analyses handing back floats they ingested.

## What to cut from the deck

- Tables 1-8 in full. Keep one row of each contrast, put the rest in the repo.
- The DFS-vs-Uniform-vs-Count ordering: the evidence estimate is flat at this
  density (τ CV 0.08-0.17), the arms differ by less than the seed spread, and
  the ordering has now flipped twice. Say that once and stop reporting it as a
  result until there is a redundancy regime to test it in.
- The lead-6/lead-0 column: every arm reads 1.01-1.05× because every arm is
  close to climatology at every lead.
