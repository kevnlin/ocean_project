## Slide 2 — Why numbers sat near 1: runs that collapse on one seed

Show `fig_loss_curves.png` (right panel).

* The D4RT refiner starts with a **3,500 km** Gaussian and a **0.05** gate.
* On seed 1235, **6 of 12** arms with that initialisation ended near the
  climatology (J 0.96-0.97), including the baseline and the new backbone; on
  seed 1234 the same arms reach J 0.86-0.88.
* A collapsed run moves an arm by ~0.1 z, more than any switch, so single-seed
  rankings are not readable.
* **Fix:** start the refiner local (150 km / 100 m) or open its gate (1.0). Both
  seeds then land together at J 0.86-0.87 (`refiner_local`, `refiner_gate1`,
  `fixed_stack`).

## Slide 5 — The backbone: PhCA-style (LNO) vs Perceiver-IO

| (D4RT decoder + DFS in both) | seed 1234 | seed 1235 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Perceiver-IO + DFS | 0.9264 | 1.0172 *collapsed* |
| Perceiver-IO + uniform mass | 0.9262 | 0.9584 |
| **PhCA-style (LNO), 32 slots, DFS** | **0.9251** | 1.0365 *collapsed* |
| PhCA-style + uniform mass | 0.9756 | 0.9988 |

**Say:** a tie. Same size (405,543 vs 405,063), and the same seed collapses:
swapping the fuse does not remove it. DFS beat uniform inside PhCA on the one
clean seed (−0.05 z): worth re-testing once the refiner is fixed. No speed-up
here (4.1 h vs 3.9 h): wall time is the DFS neighbour search, not attention.
Note for the paper: we use a PhCA-*style* fuse — LNO's position-only attention
with Slot-Attention normalisation, so DFS stays conserved.

## Slide 6 — What the ablations say (`fig_ablation.png`)

Where neither run collapsed (the clean column of `ablation_ladder.md`):

* **help:** refiner locality / gate (−0.011 z, and they end the collapse), input
  QC (salinity J 0.906 → 0.876), per-level tokens (−0.017 at cap 1000);
* **hurt:** a 1000-profile cap (+0.039), count mass instead of DFS (+0.020),
  uniform mass inside the PhCA fuse (+0.051);
* **no effect:** DFS vs uniform in the Perceiver (−0.0002), the PhCA fuse itself
  (−0.0013), the climatology position.
