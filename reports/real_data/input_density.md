**Consequence for the tables.** The arm labelled 128 delivers a mean of roughly 81-89 profiles in training and 87-117 at evaluation, so the density contrast is about 24 vs 85, not 24 vs 128. The 24 arm does reach its cap in essentially every month, so only the upper arm is overstated by its label.
