# Forecast skill by lead, CESM2, Argo + surface inputs

Zihao's shared T/S alignment model at the medium size (128 latent slots, width 256), Argo plus SST, SSS and steric SSH, the local OI-error scale by depth × 5° cell, 70,000 steps of 1,024 queries. One model for every lead: the inputs are the floats and satellite fields of month t, the target is the T/S field at the query profiles of month t + lead. Lead 0 is reconstruction. The training windows end in 2003 (4 years: 2000–2003, 40 years: 1964–2003); the normalisation, climatology, OI anchor and scale table are the 2000–2003 fits for both. Two baselines at every lead: persisted OI (the frozen OI of month t used as the field of month t + lead) and climatology (a zero anomaly), which is the better of the two at long leads. Gains are against the better one.

RMSE on every query cell of the 2004–2005 target months, mean ± standard deviation over seeds.

Seeds finished: 4 years 3, 40 years 3.
