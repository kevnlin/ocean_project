# Forecast skill by lead, CESM2, Argo + surface inputs

Zihao's shared T/S alignment model at the medium size (128 latent slots, width 256), Argo plus SST, SSS and steric SSH, the local OI-error scale by depth × 5° cell, 70,000 steps of 1,024 queries. One model for every lead: the inputs are the floats and satellite fields of month t, the target is the T/S field at the query profiles of month t + lead. Lead 0 is reconstruction. The training windows end in 2003 (4 years: 2000–2003, 40 years: 1964–2003); the normalisation, climatology, OI anchor and scale table are the 2000–2003 fits for both. The baseline at every lead is persisted OI: the frozen OI of month t used as the field of month t + lead.
